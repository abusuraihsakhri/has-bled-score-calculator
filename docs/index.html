Interpretation and limitations

A score of 3 or more identifies elevated bleeding risk and prompts review of modifiable factors. A high score alone is not a reason to withhold oral anticoagulation. Historical rates are cohort-level observations, not individualized predictions. Rates above score 5 are not reported because observations were insufficient.

Labile INR primarily applies to vitamin K antagonist therapy. This tool does not calculate stroke risk, net clinical benefit, or treatment eligibility. Clinical decisions require current guidelines and individual evaluation.

References: Pisters et al., Chest (2010); 2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS AF guideline.
